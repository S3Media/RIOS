How It Works

Find a Plan. We’ll Help Determine if It Fits.

Our collection of permit-ready house plans is designed to give you a starting point for your next project. Browse our library, find homes that fit your style and needs, and then let our architectural team help determine which plans are appropriate for your property.

01

Browse Our Plans

Explore our collection of Florida-focused house plans and find designs that inspire you.

  • Use our plan filters to narrow your search by square footage, number of stories, bedrooms, parking, buildable dimensions, architectural style, and more.
  • Found a few you like? Great. Save or note the plans that best match your vision.

02

Submit Your Property Information

Once you have an idea of what you like, complete our short project questionnaire and provide your property’s survey and available site information.

Project questionnaire includes:

  • Contact information (name, company, email, phone)
  • Have any plans caught your eye? (yes / no / not sure + open text)
  • Your project wants (square footage, bedrooms, bathrooms, stories, style)
  • Your property (address, survey upload, known site conditions/constraints)

Please note: A current Boundary, Topographic, and Tree (BTT) Survey is required for a complete feasibility review. Without a BTT Survey, we can provide a preliminary assessment only and cannot confirm feasibility.

Our team will review your property and preferences, considering:

  • Lot dimensions and buildable area
  • Property setbacks and site constraints
  • Flood zone considerations
  • Building orientation and placement
  • Parking requirements
  • Plan dimensions and overall fit
  • Other site-specific considerations

Feasibility Review & Plan Recommendations — $500

Once payment is received, our team will complete the feasibility review and provide you with a curated list of house plans that may work for your property.

Estimated timeline: 1 week

03

Select Your Plan & Receive Your Permit-Ready Drawings

After reviewing the plans recommended for your property, select the design that best fits your project wants/needs. Once you approve your selection, our architectural team will prepare the signed and sealed permit-ready drawings.

Signed & Sealed Permit-Ready Plans — $3,500

Our standard turnaround is approximately 2 weeks after plan selection and approval. The $3,500 fee includes:

  • Signed & sealed permit-ready architectural drawings
  • Preparation based on the selected existing plan
  • Little to no design changes*
  • Two rounds of permit comments

*Please note: Site adaptations that do not affect the design of the selected plan are included. While modifications to the selected plan can be accommodated, extensive changes are discouraged in order to maintain the functionality of the original design and the competitive pricing of the plan. Modifications exceeding one hour of design time will be billed at the Firm’s standard hourly rate.
