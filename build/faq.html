General

What are permit-ready house plans?

Our permit-ready house plans are pre-designed architectural plans. Each plan is designed with Florida projects in mind and can be evaluated for suitability to your specific property.

Can I purchase a house plan directly from the website?

Our website is designed as a library of house plans, rather than a traditional online plan store. You can browse our collection, save your favorites, and use the plans as inspiration for your project.

Once you find plans you’re interested in, our team will review your property and help determine which options may be a good fit before moving forward with your signed and sealed drawings.

Do I have to choose a plan before starting the process?

Not at all. You are welcome to browse the library and identify plans that catch your eye, but you can also start with your project needs and property information. Our team can help identify plans that may work well for your lot.

Can I choose any plan from the library for my property?

Absolutely. Your selected plans can serve as a starting point for your project. Because every property is different, we’ll review your lot and project requirements to determine whether the plan is appropriate or if another option may be a better fit.

Property & Feasibility

What information do I need to provide to get started?

To begin, you’ll complete our project questionnaire and provide your property’s current Boundary, Topographic, and Tree (BTT) Survey, along with any additional site information or known conditions you’d like our team to consider.

Why do I need a BTT Survey?

A current BTT Survey allows our team to evaluate important characteristics of your property, including lot dimensions, topography, trees, setbacks, and other site conditions that may affect the placement and feasibility of a plan.

Can you review my property without a BTT Survey?

We can provide a preliminary assessment without a BTT Survey; however, we cannot confirm feasibility without current survey information. For the most accurate review, an up-to-date BTT Survey is required.

What does the feasibility review include?

Our team will review your property and project preferences to identify plans that may be appropriate for your lot. The review may consider:

  • Lot dimensions and buildable area
  • Property setbacks and site constraints
  • Flood zone considerations
  • Building orientation and placement
  • Parking requirements
  • Plan dimensions and overall fit
  • Other applicable site-specific considerations
Does the feasibility review guarantee that my selected plan will be approved?

The feasibility review is intended to identify plans that appear appropriate based on the property information and survey provided. Final feasibility and approval are still subject to applicable jurisdictional requirements, site conditions, permitting authorities, and other project-specific factors.

03

The Feasibility Review

How much does the feasibility review cost?

The Feasibility Review & Plan Recommendations service is $500. This covers the review of your property and project requirements and the identification of house plans that may be appropriate for your lot.

What do I receive after the feasibility review?

You’ll receive a curated list of house plans that our team has identified as potential options for your property based on your survey, project preferences, and site conditions.

How long does the feasibility review take?

Our estimated turnaround is approximately one week after we receive the required information and payment.

What happens after I receive my recommended plans?

You’ll review the recommended options and select the plan that best fits your project. Once your plan is selected and approved, we can move forward with preparing the signed and sealed permit-ready drawings.

What happens if no plan works?

Our team will identify plans that appear to be the best fit based on the information available. If none of the available plans are appropriate for your property, we can discuss alternative architectural services to help meet your project’s needs.

04

Signed & Sealed Permit-Ready Plans

How much do the signed and sealed plans cost?

The fee for the signed and sealed permit-ready architectural drawings is $3,500.

What is included in the $3,500 fee?

The $3,500 fee includes:

  • Signed and sealed permit-ready architectural drawings
  • Preparation based on the selected existing plan
  • Site adaptations that do not affect the design of the selected plan
  • Little to no design modifications
  • Two rounds of permit comments
How long does it take to receive the signed and sealed plans?

The estimated turnaround is approximately two weeks after plan selection and approval, assuming all required information has been received.

Are modifications to the selected plan allowed?

Modifications can be accommodated; however, the program is designed around selecting an existing plan with little to no changes. Extensive modifications are discouraged in order to maintain the functionality of the original design and the competitive pricing of the plan.

What if I want significant changes to the plan?

Modifications exceeding one hour of design time will be billed at the Firm’s standard hourly rate. Significant redesigns may also require additional architectural services and fees.

What are site adaptations?

Site adaptations are adjustments needed to appropriately place the selected plan on your property that do not alter the fundamental design of the plan. These adaptations are included in the $3,500 fee.

What if I change my mind about the plan after selecting it?

Once a plan has been selected and approved for the signed and sealed drawing phase, changing to a different plan may require additional review and fees. We recommend carefully reviewing your recommended options before making your final selection.

05

Permitting

Does the $3,500 fee include permit comments?

Yes. The $3,500 fee includes two rounds of permit comments.

What happens if the plans receive more than two rounds of permit comments?

Additional permit revisions beyond the two included rounds may be billed at the Firm’s standard hourly rate.

Does Signed House Plans handle the permitting process?

The signed and sealed drawings are provided to the client for permitting. The client or their designated contractor/permit expediter is responsible for submitting the plans to the applicable jurisdiction.

Are engineering services included?

Engineering and other consultant services are not included in the $3,500 architectural plan fee and may be required separately depending on the project and jurisdiction.
